About

Hi, welcome to my website! I'm Kevin, a data scientist with a PhD in astrophysics.

I grew up in Luxembourg and did a bachelor's in physics and a master's in astrophysics at the University of Bonn, Germany. During the final year of my master's, I also completed Dataquest's Data Scientist in Python path, and I've continued building my skills in Python, SQL, statistics and machine learning ever since.

After my studies, I returned home and worked for six months as a BI developer at Delta IT, where I used SQL to build and maintain database views connected to the company's ERP platform, and created dashboards and charts to track financial, buying and selling KPIs for clients. Working directly with clients to understand what they needed taught me how to communicate data clearly to non-technical people.

I then moved to Australia for a PhD in astrophysics at the University of Melbourne, where I was part of the South Pole Telescope (SPT) Collaboration. My work focused on gravitational lensing of the cosmic microwave background, which measures how the gravity of matter bends the faint afterglow of the Big Bang. I led the development of two end-to-end Python pipelines. Working on these projects helped me improve my programming and statistical skills and taught me how to run code efficiently on high-performance computing clusters. My research resulted in two first-author publications.

I'm currently looking for jobs involving machine learning in industry or academia. If you're looking for someone with a strong quantitative background, feel free to reach out!

Outside of work, I like working out, playing guitar, applying machine learning methods to topics I find interesting, and watching a good movie or TV show. I also enjoy going for hikes, travelling whenever I get the chance, and trying out something new.
