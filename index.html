ABOUT ME

I'm a data scientist with a PhD in astrophysics from the University of Melbourne, where I was part of the South Pole Telescope (SPT) Collaboration. I led the development of two end-to-end Python data pipelines, which resulted in two first-author papers. I also have experience working as a BI developer, using SQL and data visualisation to help clients understand their business data. Through my research and professional experience, I've developed a strong quantitative background and experience communicating technical results to both technical and non-technical audiences.

I'm currently looking for opportunities involving machine learning in industry or academia. If you're looking for someone with a strong quantitative background, feel free to reach out!

Outside of work, I like working out, playing guitar, applying machine learning methods to topics I find interesting, and watching a good movie or TV show. I also enjoy going for hikes, travelling whenever I get the chance, and trying out something new.
